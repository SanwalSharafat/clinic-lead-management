# BrightSmile Dental Clinic — Services

We offer a comprehensive range of dental services to meet the needs of every member of your family. Below is a detailed overview of each service, including pricing, duration, and guidance on when to consider it.

---

## General Dentistry

### Routine Dental Cleaning
- **Price:** $120 – $180
- **Duration:** 45 minutes
- **Description:** Professional cleaning (prophylaxis) to remove plaque and tartar buildup above and below the gumline, followed by polishing.
- **When to consider:** Every 6 months as part of your preventive care routine, or sooner if you notice persistent bad breath, gum sensitivity, or visible tartar.

### Dental Exam
- **Price:** $80 – $120
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Description:** A thorough visual examination of your teeth, gums, and oral tissues by one of our dentists, including a review of your dental history and any concerns.
- **When to consider:** At least twice yearly, or whenever you experience tooth pain, sensitivity, or changes in your mouth.

### Dental X-Rays
- **Price:** $100 – $250 (depending on type and number of images)
- **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Description:** Digital radiographs to detect cavities, bone loss, impacted teeth, and other issues not visible during a standard exam. We use low-radiation digital sensors for your safety.
- **When to consider:** Typically taken once a year during your routine checkup, or as needed for diagnostic purposes.

---

## Preventive Care

### Fluoride Treatment
- **Price:** $50 – $80
- **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Description:** A concentrated fluoride gel or varnish applied to the teeth to strengthen enamel and help prevent cavities.
- **When to consider:** Especially beneficial for children and teenagers, adults with a history of cavities, or patients with dry mouth or receding gums.

### Dental Sealants
- **Price:** $60 – $120 per tooth
- **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Description:** A thin protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of the back teeth (molars) to shield them from decay.
- **When to consider:** Ideal for children and young adults shortly after their permanent molars erupt. Adults with deep grooves in their molars may also benefit.

### Oral Cancer Screening
- **Price:** $75 – $150
- **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Description:** A visual and tactile examination of the lips, tongue, throat, and oral tissues to detect early signs of oral cancer. May include a special light or rinse-based screening tool.
- **When to consider:** Recommended annually for all adult patients, especially those who use tobacco or alcohol, or have a family history of oral cancer.

## Restorative Dentistry

### Fillings
- **Price:** $150 – $400 per tooth (depending on material and cavity size)
- **Duration:** 30 – 60 minutes
- **Description:** Removal of decayed tooth structure and placement of a tooth-colored composite filling to restore the tooth's shape and function.
- **When to consider:** When a cavity is detected during an exam or X-ray, or if you experience sensitivity in a specific tooth.

### Root Canal Therapy
- **Price:** $800 – $1,500 (depending on tooth location and complexity)
- **Duration:** 60 – 90 minutes
- **Description:** Removal of infected or damaged pulp from inside the tooth, followed by cleaning, disinfecting, and sealing the root canals to save the natural tooth.
- **When to consider:** When you experience severe tooth pain, prolonged sensitivity to hot or cold, swelling, or a tooth that has become darkened or tender to the touch.

### Dental Crowns
- **Price:** $1,000 – $1,800
- **Duration:** Two visits (first visit: 60 min for preparation; second visit: 30 min for placement)
- **Description:** A custom-made cap that covers a damaged, weakened, or misshapen tooth to restore its strength, appearance, and function. Crowns are crafted from high-quality porcelain or ceramic materials.
- **When to consider:** After a root canal, for a cracked or severely worn tooth, or to cover a large filling that no longer supports the tooth structure.

### Dental Bridges
- **Price:** $2,000 – $4,000
- **Duration:** Two visits (similar to crown process)
- **Description:** A fixed prosthetic that replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the adjacent natural teeth (abutments). Bridges restore your bite and prevent remaining teeth from shifting.
- **When to consider:** When you have one or more missing teeth and the adjacent teeth are healthy enough to support a bridge.

### Dentures
- **Price:** Partial dentures $1,500 – $3,000 | Full dentures $3,000 – $6,000
- **Duration:** Multiple visits over 3 – 5 weeks
- **Description:** Custom-fitted removable prosthetics to replace missing teeth. Partial dentures fill gaps between remaining natural teeth; full dentures replace an entire arch of teeth.
- **When to consider:** When multiple or all teeth are missing and implants or bridges are not suitable. We ensure a comfortable fit and natural appearance.

## Cosmetic Dentistry

### Teeth Whitening
- **Price:** In-office: $400 – $600 | Take-home kit: $200 – $300
- **Duration:** In-office: 60 – 90 minutes | Take-home: 2 – 3 weeks of daily use
- **Description:** Professional whitening to lighten stained or discolored teeth. In-office treatment uses a high-concentration gel with a special light for immediate results. Take-home kits include custom-fitted trays and professional-grade whitening gel.
- **When to consider:** If your teeth are healthy but you'd like a brighter smile. Not recommended for patients with active decay or gum disease.

### Porcelain Veneers
- **Price:** $800 – $2,000 per tooth
- **Duration:** Two visits (first visit: 45 – 60 min for preparation and impressions; second visit: 30 – 45 min for bonding)
- **Description:** Thin, custom-crafted porcelain shells bonded to the front surface of teeth to correct chips, gaps, severe staining, or minor misalignment.
- **When to consider:** When you want a dramatic, long-lasting improvement to the appearance of your smile and your teeth are otherwise healthy.

### Dental Bonding
- **Price:** $200 – $600 per tooth
- **Duration:** 30 – 60 minutes per tooth
- **Description:** A tooth-colored composite resin applied and shaped to repair chipped, cracked, or discolored teeth in a single visit.
- **When to consider:** For minor cosmetic repairs such as small chips, gaps, or discoloration. A more affordable alternative to veneers for simple cases.

## Orthodontics

### Invisalign Consultation
- **Price:** Free
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Description:** An evaluation with Dr. Priya Sharma to determine if Invisalign clear aligners are a good fit for your orthodontic needs, including a digital scan and treatment preview.
- **When to consider:** If you're interested in straightening your teeth discreetly without traditional metal braces.

### Invisalign Treatment
- **Price:** $3,000 – $6,000 (depending on complexity)
- **Duration:** 12 – 18 months
- **Description:** A series of custom clear aligners that gradually shift your teeth into proper alignment. Includes regular progress check-ins every 6 – 8 weeks.
- **When to consider:** For mild to moderate orthodontic issues including crowding, spacing, and bite alignment in teens and adults.

---

## Emergency Dental Care

### Emergency Exam
- **Price:** $100 – $200
- **Duration:** Same-day availability
- **Description:** A prompt examination for acute dental issues such as severe pain, knocked-out teeth, broken teeth, or swelling. Includes any necessary X-rays and a treatment plan.
- **When to consider:** Anytime you experience sudden, severe dental pain, trauma, or swelling that cannot wait for a regular appointment.

### Tooth Extraction
- **Price:** $200 – $500 (simple); surgical extractions may be higher and require referral
- **Duration:** 30 – 60 minutes
- **Description:** Removal of a tooth that cannot be saved. Simple extractions are performed in-office; complex cases may be referred to an oral surgeon.
- **When to consider:** When a tooth is severely decayed, infected beyond repair, or causing crowding issues.
