# BrightSmile Dental Clinic — Frequently Asked Questions

## Insurance & Payments

**What insurance plans do you accept?**
We accept most major dental insurance plans, including:
- Delta Dental
- Cigna
- Aetna
- MetLife
- Guardian
- United Healthcare
- Blue Cross Blue Shield

If your plan is not listed, please call us at +1 (217) 555-0142 and we will verify your benefits for you. We also recommend contacting your insurance provider directly to confirm in-network status.

**What payment options do you accept?**
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express), and offer financing through **CareCredit**. For treatments totaling over $500, we also offer **in-house payment plans** with 0% interest over 3 to 6 months. Ask our office manager, Diane, for details.

**Do you accept Medicaid?**
No, BrightSmile Dental Clinic does not currently accept Medicaid. However, we do offer a **15% self-pay discount** for patients without insurance to help make care more accessible.

## Your First Visit

**What should I bring to my first appointment?**
Please bring the following:
- A valid photo ID (driver's license, state ID, or passport)
- Your dental insurance card
- A list of any current medications (including dosages)
- Completed new patient forms (available on our website at www.brightsmile-springfield.com)

If you are unable to complete the forms online, please arrive **15 minutes early** to fill them out in our office.

**How long will my first visit take?**
Expect your first visit to last approximately **45 to 60 minutes**. This includes a comprehensive dental exam, any necessary X-rays, and a thorough discussion of your treatment plan and next steps.

**How often should I visit the dentist?**
We recommend a routine cleaning and exam **every 6 months** for most patients. Some patients with gum disease or other conditions may need more frequent visits — your dentist will advise you based on your individual needs.

## Cancellations

**What is your cancellation policy?**
We require **at least 24 hours' notice** for any appointment cancellation or reschedule. Late cancellations or no-shows may incur a **$50 fee**. We understand that emergencies happen — please call us as soon as possible so we can offer the slot to another patient.

## Sedation & Comfort

**Do you offer sedation dentistry?**
Yes. We offer **nitrous oxide (laughing gas)** for patients who experience dental anxiety or are undergoing longer procedures. Nitrous oxide is safe, effective, and wears off quickly so you can drive yourself home afterward. There is an additional fee of **$50 – $75 per session**. Please let us know when scheduling if you would like nitrous oxide available during your visit.

## After-Hours Emergencies

**What should I do if I have a dental emergency after hours?**
Call our main number at **+1 (217) 555-0142**. You will be directed to our **on-call dentist** via voicemail. The on-call dentist can provide guidance over the phone and determine whether you need to be seen urgently. For life-threatening emergencies (difficulty breathing, uncontrolled bleeding), please call 911 or visit your nearest emergency room.
