## Walk-In Policy

Walk-in patients are accepted **Monday through Friday before 10:00 AM for dental emergencies only** (severe pain, trauma, swelling, or a knocked-out tooth). For all non-emergency needs — including cleanings, exams, and consultations — an appointment is required.

## Appointment Scheduling

- Appointments are available in **30-minute or 60-minute blocks**, starting on the hour and half-hour (e.g., 8:00, 8:30, 9:00, 9:30, etc.).
- **First available appointments** for non-emergency care are typically within **1 to 3 business days**. During peak periods, it may take up to 5 business days.
- We recommend scheduling your next appointment at checkout to secure your preferred time slot.

## Same-Day Emergency Slots

- We reserve **2 to 3 same-day emergency slots** each weekday, available on a **first-come, first-served basis**.
- Emergency slots fill quickly, so we encourage patients to call as early as possible.
- If all same-day slots are full, our team will help arrange the earliest available appointment and provide guidance on managing pain or discomfort in the meantime.

## Holiday Closures

The clinic is closed on the following holidays:

- New Year's Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day (July 4th)
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Eve
- Christmas Day

When a holiday falls on a Saturday, the clinic will be closed the preceding Friday. When a holiday falls on a Sunday, the clinic will be closed the following Monday. We will notify patients of any schedule changes at least two weeks in advance.
