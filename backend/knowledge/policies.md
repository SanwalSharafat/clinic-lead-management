# BrightSmile Dental Clinic — Office Policies

## New Patient Forms

New patient forms are available on our website at **www.brightsmile-springfield.com**. We encourage you to complete them online before your appointment to save time. If you prefer to fill them out in person, please **arrive 15 minutes early** to allow enough time before your scheduled appointment.

---

## Minors & Guardian Consent

- Patients **under the age of 18** must be accompanied by a **parent or legal guardian** for their first visit.
- The parent or legal guardian must sign all required consent forms, including the treatment consent and HIPAA acknowledgment.
- For subsequent visits, a parent or guardian is not required to be present in the treatment room but must provide written authorization if someone other than a parent/guardian will accompany the minor.

---

## Payment Policy

- **Co-payments** are due at the time of service.
- For insured patients, the remaining balance will be billed directly to your insurance provider. You will be notified of any patient responsibility (deductible, co-insurance, or non-covered services) after insurance processing.
- **Self-pay patients** are asked to pay the full amount at the time of service. A 15% self-pay discount is available for those without insurance.
- **Payment plans** are available for treatments totaling over $500. These are **0% interest** plans with terms of **3 to 6 months**, managed through our office. Approval is subject to a brief application. Speak with our office manager, Diane, for more information.

---

## HIPAA & Patient Privacy

BrightSmile Dental Clinic strictly complies with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Your personal health information is protected at all times and will **not be shared with any third party without your explicit written consent**, except as required by law. Our full Notice of Privacy Practices is provided to all new patients and is available upon request at any time.

---

## Late Arrival Policy

If you arrive **15 minutes or more late** for your appointment, we may need to **reschedule** you to a later time or another day. This policy ensures that every patient receives their full allotted time and that our schedule runs smoothly for all. If you are running behind, please call us so we can assess whether we can still accommodate you.

---

## Referral Policy

When a patient's needs exceed the scope of our in-house services (e.g., oral surgery, periodontics, pediatric specialty care), our team will coordinate a **referral to a trusted specialist**. Dental records and X-rays will be transferred to the specialist with your written consent. We follow up after your specialist visit to ensure continuity of care and to update your treatment plan accordingly.

---

## Treatment Plan Changes

Your treatment plan is a collaborative agreement between you and your dentist. **Any changes to the agreed-upon plan will be discussed with you in advance**, including any impact on cost or timeline. We will never perform an additional or different procedure without your informed consent. If new findings during treatment require a change in approach, your dentist will pause, explain the situation, and obtain your approval before proceeding.

---

## Medical Records Requests

Patients have the right to request copies of their dental records at any time. Requests should be submitted in writing (in person or via email to info@brightsmile-springfield.com). Records are typically processed within **5 to 7 business days**. A nominal fee may apply for printing and mailing physical copies; digital copies are provided free of charge.

---

*If you have any questions about these policies, please don't hesitate to ask our team. We're here to help.*
