# BrightSmile Dental Clinic — Our Doctors & Team

## Dentists

### Dr. Sarah Mitchell, DDS
- **Specialty:** General & Cosmetic Dentistry
- **Experience:** 15 years
- **Schedule:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday
- **About:** Dr. Mitchell is the founding dentist of BrightSmile Dental Clinic. She earned her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree from the University of Illinois College of Dentistry and has been serving the Springfield community since 2010. Dr. Mitchell is passionate about cosmetic transformations — from whitening and veneers to full smile makeovers — while maintaining a strong foundation in preventive and general dentistry. Patients appreciate her calm, reassuring chairside manner and her commitment to making every visit comfortable.

### Dr. James Rodriguez, DMD
- **Specialty:** Restorative & Endodontics
- **Experience:** 12 years
- **Schedule:** Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday
- **About:** Dr. Rodriguez specializes in restoring damaged and decayed teeth, with particular expertise in root canal therapy, crowns, bridges, and dentures. He earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine degree from Southern Illinois University School of Dental Medicine. Known for his gentle technique with anxious patients, Dr. Rodriguez takes pride in saving teeth that other practices might recommend extracting. He is fluent in both English and Spanish.

### Dr. Priya Sharma, DDS
- **Specialty:** Orthodontics & Invisalign
- **Experience:** 10 years
- **Schedule:** Monday through Thursday
- **About:** Dr. Sharma is our in-house orthodontic specialist. She earned her DDS and completed her orthodontic residency at the University of Michigan. She focuses exclusively on orthodontic care, including Invisalign clear aligner therapy and comprehensive bite correction. **Please note:** Dr. Sharma sees orthodontic patients only and does not perform general checkups or routine cleanings.
